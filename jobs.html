Software Engineer

Reference: SE204

Build and maintain reliable web applications for customers and colleagues.

Salary: £42,000–£52,000 per year

Reports to: Engineering Manager

Key responsibilities

  1. Develop, test and maintain application features.
  2. Review code and collaborate with product and design teams.
  3. Investigate technical issues and document solutions.

Requirements

  • Essential: Experience with JavaScript and a server-side language.
  • Essential: Familiarity with version control and software testing.
  • Preferable: Experience with cloud platforms or containerisation.

Data Analyst

Reference: DA517

Analyse business data and communicate insights that support sound decisions.

Salary: £36,000–£44,000 per year

Reports to: Head of Business Intelligence

Key responsibilities

  • Prepare, validate and analyse data from business systems.
  • Create reports and dashboards for stakeholders.
  • Document data definitions and analysis methods.

Requirements

  • Essential: Practical SQL skills and experience analysing data.
  • Essential: Clear communication and attention to detail.
  • Preferable: Experience with Power BI, Tableau or similar tools.
